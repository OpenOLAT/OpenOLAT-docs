# Requirements when Working with OpenOlat {: #requirements}

:octicons-device-camera-video-24: **Video Introduction (German)**: [Requirements](<https://www.youtube.com/embed/RJ2Q9fSG-ww>){:target="_blank"}

OpenOlat is a web application. In order to work with OpenOlat you will need an internet connection as well as a modern web browser. Please perform a [browser check](../login_registration/Login_Page.md#browsercheck) to be sure that your browser will support all of OpenOlat's features.

Because OpenOlat is a web application, access is location- and device-independent. You simply log in through the browser, whether on your workplace PC, at home, on a laptop or on a mobile phone. Your learning progress, for example completed course elements, test results and assessments, is stored server-side with your user account and is not tied to a specific device. When you switch devices, you log in with the same credentials and continue seamlessly. Whether an OpenOlat instance is also reachable from outside the institution's network, for example from home, depends on the settings of the operating institution.

If you want to offer courses in OpenOlat, you first have to apply for author rights. To do so, contact your local support team. As an OpenOlat author you can create online courses and other learning resources on your own. For this purpose, the [authoring](../area_modules/Authoring.md) area is available to you with a wide range of options.

Plan your didactic concept, create your learning content and transfer everything into the structures and options of OpenOlat.
