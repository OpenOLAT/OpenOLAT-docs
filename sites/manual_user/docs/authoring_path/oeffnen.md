# Open {: #oeffnen}

<p class="oo-mh-sub">Access and members</p>

The course gets members and a way in: through the access configuration, an offer in the catalog or a group. Three course roles share the work.

## Terms of this station {: #terms}

<div class="oo-mh-terms" markdown>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Concept</p>

### Access configuration {: #course_access_configuration}

The area of a learning resource where the offers are set: who may book, at what price and in which period.

??? note "Access configuration in detail"

    The area of a learning resource where the offers are set: who may book, at what price and in which period.

    **German:** Zugangskonfiguration

    [Read in the manual](../learningresources/Access_configuration.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Access%20configuration%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Concept</p>

### Offer {: #catalog_offer}

The definition of the conditions under which a learning resource can be booked: for whom, in which period, at what price and in what way.

??? note "Offer in detail"

    The definition of the conditions under which a learning resource can be booked: for whom, in which period, at what price and in what way. One resource can carry several offers side by side.

    **German:** Angebot

    **What users call it:** offer, booking option

    [Read in the manual](../area_modules/catalog2.0_angebote.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Offer%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Module</p>

### Catalog {: #catalog_catalog}

The module that puts learning resources and implementations with an offer on display for booking.

??? note "Catalog in detail"

    The module that puts learning resources and implementations with an offer on display for booking. It is structured through the taxonomy and the launchers. Without an offer a resource does not appear. Without signing in it is reachable as the web catalog, if that is switched on.

    **German:** Katalog

    **What users call it:** catalogue, course catalog, offering

    [Read in the manual](../../manual_admin/administration/Modules_Catalog_2.0.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Catalog%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Concept</p>

### Group {: #collaboration_group}

A group of people who work together, with members, tools and a lifecycle of their own.

??? note "Group in detail"

    A group of people who work together, with members, tools and a lifecycle of their own. It can exist inside a course or independently of any course in the Groups area.

    **German:** Gruppe

    **What users call it:** group, team, cohort, class

    [Read in the manual](../groups/index.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Group%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Role</p>

### Owner {: #roles_owner}

A course role with full responsibility for a learning resource.

??? note "Owner in detail"

    A course role with full responsibility for a learning resource. Owners edit the content, configure the resource, publish it and grant the other course roles.

    **German:** Besitzer:in

    **What users call it:** owner, course owner

    **Not to be confused with [Coach](../basic_concepts/coach.md):** Owners manage the learning resource, coaches manage the learners in it.

    [Read in the manual](../basic_concepts/Roles.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Owner%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Role</p>

### Coach {: #roles_coach}

A course role. Coaches accompany the participants of a course or a group, see all results and assess them.

??? note "Coach in detail"

    A course role. Coaches accompany the participants of a course or a group, see all results and assess them. They do not change the configuration of the course.

    **German:** Betreuer:in

    **What users call it:** tutor, instructor, facilitator, mentor

    **Not to be confused with [Owner](../basic_concepts/Roles.md):** Owners manage the learning resource, coaches manage the learners in it.

    [Read in the manual](../basic_concepts/coach.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Coach%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Role</p>

### Participant {: #roles_participant}

A course role. Participants work through the course and see their own results only.

??? note "Participant in detail"

    A course role. Participants work through the course and see their own results only.

    **German:** Teilnehmer:in

    **What users call it:** learner, student, attendee, trainee

    [Read in the manual](../basic_concepts/Roles.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Participant%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

</div>

## Deepen once the first course is running {: #deepen}

The first course is in place. Now the things that did not matter while building pay off: the terms of this station have parts and settings that control the course more precisely.

- **Offer:** [Access code](../basic_concepts/Offer_Concepts.md), Booking order forms. All settings are on the page [Catalog 2.0 - Offers](../area_modules/catalog2.0_angebote.md).
- **Catalog:** [Launcher](../../manual_admin/administration/Modules_Catalog_2.0.md), [Web catalog](../area_modules/catalog2.0_web.md). All settings are on the page [Module Catalog](../../manual_admin/administration/Modules_Catalog_2.0.md).
- **Group:** [Learning groups](../learningresources/Members_management.md), [Learning area](../learningresources/Learning_Areas.md), [Members](../learningresources/Members_management.md), [Collaborative tools](../groups/Group_Administration.md) and more. All settings are on the page [Groups](../groups/index.md).
