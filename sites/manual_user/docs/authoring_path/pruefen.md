# Assess {: #pruefen}

<p class="oo-mh-sub">Measure performance</p>

Tests measure knowledge, self-tests are for practice. The assessment tool collects the results, the evidence of achievement sums them up permanently. Certificate and badge attest them.

### Test {: #testing_test}

The course element that embeds a test learning resource into the course.

??? note "Test in detail"

    The course element that embeds a test learning resource into the course. The result counts towards the course assessment.

    **German:** Test

    **What users call it:** exam, quiz, assessment, examination

    **Not to be confused with [Self-test](../learningresources/Course_Element_Self_Test.md):** The test is assessed and counts. The self-test serves self-checking and does not feed into the course assessment.

### Self-test {: #testing_self_test}

The course element that embeds a test learning resource for self-checking.

??? note "Self-test in detail"

    The course element that embeds a test learning resource for self-checking. The result does not feed into the course assessment, the results are stored anonymised, and the number of runs is unlimited.

    **German:** Selbsttest

    **What users call it:** practice test, mock test

    **Not to be confused with [Test](../learningresources/Course_Element_Test.md):** The test is assessed and counts. The self-test serves self-checking and does not feed into the course assessment.

    [Read in the manual](../learningresources/Course_Element_Self_Test.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Self-test%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

The area where questions are collected, tagged, released and reviewed independently of a single test.

The judgement on a person's performance in an assessable course element, expressed as a status, points or a grade.

### Evidence of achievement {: #evidence_evidence_of_achievement}

The collection of all results a person has in a course, one per assessable course element, with points, status and date.

??? note "Evidence of achievement in detail"

    The collection of all results a person has in a course, one per assessable course element, with points, status and date.

    **German:** Leistungsnachweis

    **What users call it:** transcript, record of achievement, statement

    **Not to be confused with [Certificate](../personal_menu/Certificates.md):** The evidence of achievement is the evaluation in the system and is always there. The certificate is a PDF that is issued.

    [Read in the manual](../personal_menu/Evidence_of_Achievements.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Evidence%20of%20achievement%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Concept</p>

### Certificate {: #evidence_certificate}

A PDF that certifies the successful participation in a course or the completion of a certification program, with name, date and serial number.

??? note "Certificate in detail"

    A PDF that certifies the successful participation in a course or the completion of a certification program, with name, date and serial number. The evidence of achievement shows what someone has done; the certificate attests it to the outside world. Externally earned certificates can be uploaded if the administration allows it.

    **German:** Zertifikat

    **What users call it:** diploma, certificate, credential

    **Not to be confused with [Evidence of achievement](../personal_menu/Evidence_of_Achievements.md):** The evidence of achievement is the evaluation in the system and is always there. The certificate is a PDF that is issued.

    **Not to be confused with [Badge](../personal_menu/OpenBadges.md):** The certificate is a PDF made from a template. The badge is a machine-readable Open Badge with an image and criteria.

    [Read in the manual](../personal_menu/Certificates.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Certificate%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)

</div>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Concept</p>

### Badge {: #evidence_badge}

A digital credential following the Open Badges standard, with an image, criteria and an issuing body.

??? note "Badge in detail"

    A digital credential following the Open Badges standard, with an image, criteria and an issuing body. Unlike the certificate it can be shown outside OpenOlat and checked by a machine.

    **German:** Badge

    **What users call it:** badge, micro-credential, achievement, achievements

    **Not to be confused with [Certificate](../personal_menu/Certificates.md):** The certificate is a PDF made from a template. The badge is a machine-readable Open Badge with an image and criteria.

    [Read in the manual](../personal_menu/OpenBadges.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Badge%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20use%20it%3F)
