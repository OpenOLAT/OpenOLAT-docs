Attendance and absences are recorded per implementation and element in their own Absences tab. Recording at product level is planned.

## How do I do this?

Record absences in the "Absences" tab of an implementation. The tab only appears once absences have been activated on the element.

## Prerequisites

Absence management must be activated and released for the product or the element type. Activation under `Settings tab > Options > Absences configuration`.

## Where do I find the setting

`Course Planner > Implementations > "Implementation" > Absences tab`; activation: `Settings tab > Options > Absences configuration`

## Connections

Absences are recorded per event (categories Attended, Authorized, Not excused, Dispensed). From these, the attendance rate is calculated and checked against the permitted absence rate to determine whether a course counts as attended.
