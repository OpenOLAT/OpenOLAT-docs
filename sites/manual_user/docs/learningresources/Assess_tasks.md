# Assess tasks {: #assess_tasks}

Coaches and course owners assess the tasks handed in by the participants in the assessment tool or directly in the course with the course editor closed.

You can find out in detail how tasks and group tasks are evaluated in the assessment tool in the chapter ["Assessment tool"](Assessment_tool_overview.md) → ["Assessing tasks and group tasks"](Assessing_tasks_and_group_tasks.md). This page describes assessing in the course. How you keep track of all participants in the course and check assignment and submission is described on the page [Supervise a task](Course_Element_Task/Task_Coaching.md).

## Assessing in the course {: #assess_in_course}

In the course, you assess in the tab "Participants" of the course element:<br>
`Course > "Title of the course element" > Tab "Participants"`

Which tabs, columns and actions are available to you there is described on the page [Supervise a task](Course_Element_Task/Task_Coaching.md#coach_overview).

Clicking on a person opens their workflow. There, depending on the configuration of the task, you upload feedback documents and assign points. With "Finalize assessment" or "Finalize assessment and release" you finalize the assessment, the column "Step" then shows "Graded". Participants see an assessment as soon as it is released.

You assess several persons in one go: select the rows and click on "Finalize assessment" or "Release".
