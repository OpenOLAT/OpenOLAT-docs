# Podcast: Administration {: #podcast_administration}

Whether the episodes of a podcast can be rated and commented on, who receives an e-mail for new comments and how much space the audio and video files may use, you define once in the podcast learning resource. These settings apply in all courses and course elements in which you embed the podcast learning resource. Who moderates and creates episodes in a single course, on the other hand, you define in the course element, see [Configure a podcast in the course element](Podcast_Configuration.md).

You open the podcast learning resource in two ways:

* You select the learning resource in the authoring area.
* In the course editor, you click on "Edit learning resource" in the "Podcast learning content" tab of the course element.

Owners of the learning resource, learning resource managers and administrators see the "Administration" menu of the podcast learning resource. For an external podcast, they change the address of the feed there with "Replace URL".


## Settings {: #settings}

You open the settings of the podcast learning resource under `Podcast > Administration > Settings`. The tabs appear in this order: "Metadata", "Info", "Share", "Catalog", "Quota" and "Options". "Catalog" and "Quota" only appear under the conditions given in the sections below.

### Metadata {: #tab_metadata}

Here you define the title, reference, subjects and license of the podcast learning resource. For an external podcast, the tab also shows the URL of the feed. The fields are described on the page [Course Settings - Tab Metadata](Course_Settings_Metadata.md). The field "Implementation format" only exists for courses.

### Info {: #tab_info}

Here you enter the information of the info page, for example cover image, teaser and description. The fields are described on the page [Course Settings - Tab Info](Course_Settings_Info.md).

### Share {: #tab_share}

Here you define, among other things, the usage: the default is embedding in a course, alternatively you offer the podcast as a standalone learning resource. The sections of the tab correspond to those of a course, see [Course settings - Tab Share](Course_Settings_Share/index.md).

### Catalog {: #tab_catalog}

You only see the "Catalog" tab if catalog version 1 is switched on in your instance and the podcast is used standalone. There you add the podcast learning resource to the catalog, see [Course Settings, Tab Catalog](Course_Settings/index.md#catalog).

### Quota {: #tab_quota}

Audio and video episodes quickly use a lot of storage space. Here you define how much storage space the podcast learning resource may use at most: "Quota (KB)" limits the whole learning resource, "Upload limit (KB)" a single uploaded file. Without a value of its own, the system-wide default value for blogs and podcasts applies, see [Files and Folders](../../manual_admin/administration/Files_and_Folders.md#files_and_folders_quotas).

Only administrators, system administrators, learning resource managers, user managers and roles managers see the tab "Quota".

### Options [:octicons-tag-16:{ title="from Release 19.0 (OO-7728)" }](https://track.frentix.com/issue/OO-7728) {: #tab_options}

In the section "User feedback" you define with "Posts are rateable" and "Posts are commentable" whether the episodes of the podcast can be rated with stars and commented on.

With "Push E-mail by comments" the owners of the podcast and of the courses that embed it receive an e-mail for each new comment. [:octicons-tag-16:{ title="from Release 20.1 (OO-7731)" }](https://track.frentix.com/issue/OO-7731)

For standalone use, you also define in the section "Access" which access participants in status "Finished" have. The selection corresponds to that of a course, see [Course Settings - Tab Options](Course_Settings_Options.md). [:octicons-tag-16:{ title="from Release 21.0 (OO-9298)" }](https://track.frentix.com/issue/OO-9298)
