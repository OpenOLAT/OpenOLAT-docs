# Podcast: Overview {: #overview}

With a podcast you provide audio and video contributions as a series of episodes. In OpenOlat, podcasts are learning resources that you use standalone or embedded in a course.

## Profile

Name | Podcast
---------|----------
Icon | :o_icon_o_FileResource-PODCAST_icon:
Available since | Release 7.1 or earlier
Purpose | Provide audio and video contributions as a series of episodes
In the course | Course element "Podcast"
Specialty / Note | A podcast is created in OpenOlat or comes as an external podcast via an RSS feed

There are two types of podcasts:<br>
a) podcasts that are created in OpenOlat, and<br>
b) external podcasts from the internet, which are integrated via RSS feed.

A podcast usually consists of several episodes. An episode can be an audio or video contribution.

How to create a podcast for a course is described in the guide [How do I create a podcast?](../../../manual_how-to/podcast/podcast.md). How to set up the course element is described on the page [Configure a podcast in the course element](../Podcast_Configuration.md).


## General information about podcasts {: #general}

The term "Podcasting" combines the words "iPod" and "Broadcasting" and means producing and providing audio and video files. Podcasts can be subscribed to: you download the episodes to mobile devices via services such as iTunes, or you listen to and watch them directly in OpenOlat. This way, participants also use the episodes on the move, and unlike a radio show, production and use do not depend on a broadcasting time. The latest episode is always at the top. If ratings and comments are switched on in the learning resource, participants rate episodes and write comments on them.


## The learning resource podcast {: #learning_resource}

You create the podcast learning resource in the authoring area. Under `Authoring > My entries` you see, among other things, all podcasts that you have created yourself or of which you are an owner.

If you only use the podcast in a course, the usage "Embedding" is sufficient, with which OpenOlat creates every new podcast. You find it under `Podcast > Administration > Settings > Share`, see [Podcast: Administration](../Podcast_Administration.md#tab_share). Who sees the episodes in the course is then determined by the members and rights of the course. You assign members of its own to the podcast under `Podcast > Administration > Members management`, and you set up bookings under `Podcast > Administration > Offer types`. You only need both if you offer the podcast with the usage "Standalone".

The course element shows the podcast regardless of the status of the learning resource, including the status "Preparation". If the learning resource is in the recycle bin or deleted, the course element shows the note "The podcast cannot be started because the podcast resource has been deleted." instead.
