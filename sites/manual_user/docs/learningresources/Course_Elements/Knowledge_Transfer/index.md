# Knowledge transfer {: #knowledge_transfer}

With the course elements of this group, you provide course content to your participants and structure the course. Depending on the course element, you also integrate content created outside OpenOlat, for example learning content in the CP or SCORM format or videos from Opencast and Mediasite.

In the course editor, you find the course elements in the dialog "Insert course elements" in the group "Knowledge transfer":<br>
`Course > Administration > Course editor > Insert course elements`
