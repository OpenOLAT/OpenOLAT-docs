# Roles {: #rollen}

<p class="oo-mh-sub">Accounts and organisation</p>

In user management you create accounts and assign roles. The system role System administrator configures the installation, Administrator manages the organisation. User manager and Roles manager share the work on accounts, authors build courses. Every account belongs to an organisation.

## Terms of this station {: #terms}

<div class="oo-mh-terms" markdown>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Role</p>

### System administrator {: #roles_role_sysadmin}

A system role. It opens the Administration area and thereby the system configuration.

??? note "System administrator in detail"

    A system role. It opens the Administration area and thereby the system configuration. It grants no rights on objects: without an additional organisation role a system administrator sees neither courses nor accounts.

    **German:** Systemadministrator:in

    [Read in the manual](../../manual_user/basic_concepts/Roles.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22System%20administrator%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20set%20it%20up%3F)

</div>

<div class="oo-mh-term" markdown>

<p class="oo-mh-kind">Role</p>

### Administrator {: #roles_role_administrator}

An organisation role that unites all administrative roles of one organisation.

??? note "Administrator in detail"

    An organisation role that unites all administrative roles of one organisation. It is the superuser of that organisation. Its reach ends at its own organisation and its sub-organisations; it does not act system-wide and does not open the system configuration.

    **German:** Administrator:in

    [Read in the manual](../../manual_user/basic_concepts/Roles.md) · [Ask Sophia](?sophia=What%20is%20%22Administrator%22%20in%20OpenOlat%20and%20how%20do%20I%20set%20it%20up%3F)

</div>

### User manager {: #roles_role_usermanager}

An administrative organisation role. It manages the accounts of its own organisation: create, edit, import, block and delete.

??? note "User manager in detail"

    An administrative organisation role. It manages the accounts of its own organisation: create, edit, import, block and delete.

An administrative organisation role. It grants and withdraws roles on the accounts of its own organisation.

    An administrative organisation role. It grants and withdraws roles on the accounts of its own organisation.

### Author {: #roles_role_author}

An organisation role. It allows learning resources to be created in the authoring area.

??? note "Author in detail"

    An organisation role. It allows learning resources to be created in the authoring area. Authors manage their own resources, not those of the organisation.

### Organisation {: #platform_organisation}

A unit in the structure of the platform, for example a school, an institute or a department.

??? note "Organisation in detail"

    A unit in the structure of the platform, for example a school, an institute or a department. Organisations are hierarchical, carry the roles of their members and limit what administrative roles can reach.
