# What is in OpenOlat {: #whats_in_openolat}

OpenOlat is large. This page is the map: 19 subject areas in five groups, each with one sentence on what it covers and a link to the manual page where it starts. Use it to see the whole product at a glance, and to find the chapter that answers your question.

The areas come from the OpenOlat concept map, the terminology reference behind the manual and the application. They describe the product by subject, not by menu, so one area can span several places in the interface.

<p class="oo-map__lead">Select a group in the map to see its subject areas, and an area to see what it covers. The cards below follow the selection. The way back sits in the top left corner of the map.</p>
